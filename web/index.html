How your EXE works

  1. 1Copy a paragraph, article, email, or other text to the Windows clipboard.
  2. 2Open your personalized ClipboardVoice.exe (or assign a Windows hotkey to its shortcut).
  3. 3A small floating red Stop button stays at the bottom-right while Supertonic generates and speaks your text.
  4. 4Press Stop to interrupt, or let speech finish. The application exits when the last audio sample has played, not when synthesis ends.
Your clipboard is captured immediately when you launch the EXE. The Windows reader runs locally after the first model download. The private cloning service is only needed to generate a new voice JSON.

Privacy and setup

The website never commits uploaded voice files or embeddings to GitHub. When cloning, the recording is sent to the private cloning service you configure. Treat that service as a sensitive data processor.

Your personalized EXE contains the chosen voice JSON in its downloadable file, so don't distribute private voice profiles without permission.
